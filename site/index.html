About the data. From the California Secretary of State's Cal-Access campaign finance database, updated daily. Slice totals come from each committee's reported contributions in forms 460 and 497. Donor lists typically use itemized contributions of $100 or more. Amounts are money raised, not money spent.

Donors are grouped by their names as they appear in the data, so spelling variants may appear separately. Locations are the addresses donors report, which for organizations and some individuals is an office.
